# Editorial Illustrated

Hand-drawn shapes on warm paper, muted earth-plus-jewel palette, serif type. The family that performs on social.

**Reach for it when:** Blog headers, social posts, explainers where the diagram is the hero.

> **Watch out:** The looseness must be the only loose thing — irregular strokes plus irregular spacing reads as careless.
