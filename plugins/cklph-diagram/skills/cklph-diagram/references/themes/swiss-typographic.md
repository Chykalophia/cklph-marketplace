# Swiss Typographic

No boxes at all. Hairline rules, numbered sections, tabular numerals, one accent, massive whitespace. Reads like a printed annual report.

**Reach for it when:** Metrics overviews, reference sheets, print, board packs.

> **Watch out:** Only works when content is genuinely tabular or sequential. Branching systems lose their branches.
