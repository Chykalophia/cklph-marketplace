# Editorial Isometric

Warm paper and hand-set serif over a strict 30° projection — two families that usually repel, held together by grain.

**Reach for it when:** When an infrastructure story needs warmth: case studies, retrospectives.

> **Watch out:** Warm palettes bring the three face tones closer in value — check top/left/right stay distinguishable.

## Component choices

This theme is a set of choices from [`../components/`](../components/). Those
