# Disciplined Sketch

Double-stroke wobble and hachure fill over exact alignment. Reads as thinking out loud, which paradoxically signals seniority.

**Reach for it when:** Discovery, workshops, anything you want critiqued rather than approved.

> **Watch out:** Sketch signals 'not final'. Never the register for a signed-off deliverable.
