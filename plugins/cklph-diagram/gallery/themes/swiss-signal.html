Swiss Signal

International Style discipline: strict grid, one red, flush-left everything. Predictable to the point of boring, which is the property that lowers cognitive load.

Reach for it when: Technical reference, documentation, print.

Described in words

A content pipeline in three stages: intake and triage collect and sort, review and enrich add judgement and metadata, publish and archive are the two destinations. Category is carried by shape and border pattern as well as colour.
