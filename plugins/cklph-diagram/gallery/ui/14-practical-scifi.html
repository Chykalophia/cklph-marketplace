Practical Sci-Fi

An ops console that reads as an instrument, not a film prop.

Restraint is the trick. Corner brackets, tick scales, mono type at wide tracking, one accent for alarm. No glow, no scanlines, no chrome. The sci-fi signal comes from precision, not from effects.

Watch out: Dark plus thin cyan hairlines is a real sensory-load risk over long sessions, and hairlines vanish in print. Ship a light variant alongside.
