Aurora Mesh

A pipeline floating over a soft mesh gradient.

Atmosphere with a floor under it. Three radial gradients build the field; every card sits at 93% white so text never touches raw gradient. The gradient is mood, the card is the contract.

Watch out: Mesh gradients date fast and add nothing semantically. Good for a cover or a title slide, poor for a diagram someone must study.
