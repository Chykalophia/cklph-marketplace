A pipeline with hard offset shadows and no softness anywhere.

The shadow is a rectangle. A solid offset shape, not a blur — it survives any export, scales cleanly, and costs nothing to render. Planned work is a dashed outline with no shadow, so 'not built yet' is visible at a glance.

Watch out: High-saturation fills at large area can be fatiguing. Keep the fills pale and let the black borders carry the weight, as they do here.
