Component Specimen

Every part, on one sheet, to mix and match.

Components, not themes. Ten node styles, twelve connector types, six endpoints, a five-step elevation scale, three junction markers and status chips. Pick a node style from one row and a connector from another — the point is that these compose, so a house look can be assembled rather than chosen off a shelf.

Watch out: Mixing without a rule produces mush. Choose one node style, at most two connector types, and one elevation step per diagram, then hold them for the whole set.
