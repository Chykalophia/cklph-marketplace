Isometric Wireframe

Infrastructure tiers as transparent volumes.

Ten-percent fills. Hairline edges with vertex dots and a barely-there fill, so volumes read as solid without hiding what sits behind them. Dashed leaders connect tiers without implying data flow.

Watch out: Hairlines under 1px disappear on projectors and in print. Set a 1.1px floor and test at the size it will actually be shown.
