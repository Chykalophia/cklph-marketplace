Liquid Glass

Tier zones as frosted panels over a colour field.

Barrier layers. Each panel is a solid dark fill at 55%, then a white sheen at 7%, then a specular hairline. Text contrast is measured against the barrier, not the gradient — which is the difference between glass that works and glass that fails an audit.

Watch out: Glass fails hard over a busy background: detail behind the panel makes text unreadable no matter how strong the blur. Only use it over fields you control.
