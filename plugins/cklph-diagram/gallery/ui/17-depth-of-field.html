Depth of Field

Three tiers where only the one that matters is in focus.

Blur as hierarchy. Background tiers are genuinely blurred and slightly reduced in opacity; the focal tier is sharp and scaled up. Attention lands before a single word is read.

Watch out: Blurred text is unreadable text. Only ever blur what is genuinely context — and give the same content in the prose alternative, where nothing is out of focus.
