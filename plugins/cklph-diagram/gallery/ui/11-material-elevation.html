Material Elevation

A service graph where height above the page means exposure.

Elevation as a scale, not an effect. Material 3 pairs a tonal surface shift with the shadow, so depth survives greyscale — the tint moves even when the shadow cannot be seen. Shadows here are explicit blurred rects rather than feDropShadow, which some exporters silently drop.

Watch out: Elevation must map to something real. If height means nothing, flatten it — a five-step scale used decoratively is five steps of noise.
