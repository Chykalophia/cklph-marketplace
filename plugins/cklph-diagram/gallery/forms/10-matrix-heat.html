Technique — triple encoding. Shade, glyph and printed number all carry the same value, so the grid survives greyscale printing and any colour-vision difference untouched. Marginal averages give the row a baseline to judge against.

Watch out: Sequential ramps must be monotonic in lightness or the ordering breaks. Never use a rainbow ramp for magnitude.
