Swimlane Timeline

Who is doing what, and when, on one grid.

Technique — two axes at once. Actor runs down, time runs across. Fill state encodes progress — solid is done, outlined is active, dashed is not started — and a single 'today' rule turns a plan into a status report.

Watch out: The weakest cell here is duration precision: bars imply exactness they rarely have. Label the ones that matter or readers will treat estimates as commitments.
