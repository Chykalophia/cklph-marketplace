Nested Containment

Budget split with no arrows anywhere — enclosure and area do the work.

Technique — the two cheapest channels. Containment carries hierarchy and area carries weight. Dashed outlines are groupings, solid outlines are real systems, so the border style is doing semantic work rather than decoration.

Watch out: Area is systematically misjudged by readers, so always print the number too. Never rely on size alone for a value someone will act on.
