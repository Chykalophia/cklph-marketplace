Sankey Ribbons

A funnel where the width of every band is the number of users.

Technique — quantity as geometry. Band thickness encodes volume, so the shape answers 'how much' before any label is read. Translucent overlaps let crossing flows coexist without a bridge.

Watch out: Translucency stacks: three overlapping bands can produce a fourth colour that means nothing. Cap overlap depth and keep every band's label outside the ribbon.
