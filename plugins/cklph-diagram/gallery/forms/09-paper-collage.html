Paper Collage

Three phases as taped paper cards with grain and real shadow.

Technique — material depth. A turbulence-based grain filter, a genuine cast shadow, one degree of rotation and a strip of tape. Separation comes from the material, which is why not one card needs a border.

Watch out: Grain adds visual noise by definition. Keep the alpha under about 0.06 and never lay text directly on the grainiest area.
