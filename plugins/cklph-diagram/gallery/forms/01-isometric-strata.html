Isometric Strata

Four platform layers seen at an angle instead of head-on.

Technique — depth by projection. Each plane is drawn in a 2:1 isometric projection with its own cast shadow. Stacking order becomes the message, and no arrow is needed to say 'sits on top of'.

Watch out: Isometric costs readability at small sizes and cannot be scanned linearly. Use it for an overview slide, never for something someone must trace step by step.
