Circuit Traces

Signal routing drawn like a printed circuit board.

Technique — explicit joints. Orthogonal traces on a dark board, junction pads where lines meet, vias where a signal changes layer. Every joint is drawn, so there is never ambiguity about whether two crossing lines connect.

Watch out: The dark board is the highest sensory-load option in this set. Offer a light variant, and avoid it for anyone who reads for long stretches.
