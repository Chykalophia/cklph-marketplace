Organic Mind Map

A rebrand broken down with no boxes at all.

Technique — tapered branches. Each branch is a filled outline, thick at the root and thin at the tip, so depth is felt rather than labelled. Text rides the branch instead of sitting in a container, which is what separates a real mind map from a sideways org chart.

Watch out: Radial layouts have no reading order. Fine for generative thinking, wrong for anything with a sequence or a dependency.
