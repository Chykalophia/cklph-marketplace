Transit Map

Three content routes sharing interchange stops.

Technique — Beck's grammar. Geography is thrown away; only sequence and interchange survive. Lines run at 45° only, thick and rounded; shared steps become double-ringed interchanges. Route identity is carried by colour and dash pattern.

Watch out: Only works when flows genuinely share stops. With no interchanges it is just parallel lines wearing a costume.
