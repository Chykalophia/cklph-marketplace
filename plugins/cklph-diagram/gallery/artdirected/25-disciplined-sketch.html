Disciplined Sketch

Hand-drawn feel, machine consistency — sketch mode done properly.

Double-stroke plus hachure. Every shape drawn twice with a small random jitter, the second pass thinner and lighter, and filled with diagonal hachure at low opacity. Alignment underneath stays exact.

Watch out: Sketch signals 'not final'. That is the point, and it is also why it must never be the register for a signed-off deliverable.
