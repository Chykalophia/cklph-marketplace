Motion Filmstrip

A six-second loop, drawn as its key frames.

Sequence without motion. Five frames on a timeline, each showing one more stage lit. You get the narrative of an animation in a form that prints, survives screenshots, and needs no reduced-motion fallback.

Watch out: For a feed, an actual loop will outperform this. Use the filmstrip for the documentation and the loop for the post — they are different artefacts.
