Isometric Dimensional

Extruded blocks at 30°, with three-tone faces and ground shadows.

Three-tone face shading. Top face lightest, right mid, left darkest — that single rule is what makes an extrusion read as a solid rather than three flat polygons. One soft ground shadow per block anchors it.

Watch out: Isometric gets hard to read as architectures get complex, and it cannot be scanned linearly. Overview only.
