Editorial Isometric

Warm paper and hand-set serif over a strict 30° projection.

Two families that usually repel. Isometric normally goes with cool, technical, saturated palettes. Recolouring the faces into a warm editorial ramp and adding grain lets the precision and the warmth coexist.

Watch out: The face-shading rule still has to hold. Warm palettes make the three tones closer in value, so check the top/left/right steps stay distinguishable.
