Dark Glass

The near-black product-marketing register — highest expensive-signal per pixel.

Depth from borders, not from background shifts. Near-black ground, hairline borders at 8–16% white, one radial wash behind the single focal node, a 24px grid at 2.5% opacity, mono labels at tight tracking, and grain over everything.

Watch out: Everything here rides on one accent. The moment a second saturated colour appears, the register collapses into a generic dark theme.
