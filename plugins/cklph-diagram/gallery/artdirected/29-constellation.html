Constellation

Density as shading — for graphs too big to draw as boxes.

Edges at 10% opacity. Node radius encodes centrality, colour encodes cluster rather than category, and edges are so faint that overlapping them produces the shading. Labels appear only for hubs.

Watch out: This is a picture of shape, not of detail. Never ask a reader to trace a specific path through it — pair it with a table.
