Fix 7 · Treated Ground

Untreated white is the loudest signal of a default export.

Four grounds, same node. Plain white, a 16px dot grid, a radial wash, and grain. All four are equally legible; only the first looks unconsidered.

Watch out: Keep grid and wash under about 10% opacity, and check text contrast against the treated ground rather than against the base colour.
