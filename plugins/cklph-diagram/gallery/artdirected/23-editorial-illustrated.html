Editorial Illustrated

Hand-drawn shapes on warm paper — the family that performs on social.

Irregular outline, perfect intent. Each shape is a closed path with varied control points and a second offset stroke at lower opacity, so the edge has the weight variation of a real pen. Warm paper, muted earth-plus-jewel palette, serif type, and one sentence of copy.

Watch out: The looseness has to be the only loose thing. Irregular strokes plus irregular spacing reads as careless rather than crafted.
