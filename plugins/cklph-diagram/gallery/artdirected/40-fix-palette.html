A warm neutral ramp plus two restrained accents.

The neutrals carry a tint. Every step here holds a trace of the accent hue, which is what makes a palette feel authored rather than assembled. Saturated primaries are shown beside it for contrast.

Watch out: Two accents is the ceiling. A third is almost always a category that should have been a shape.
