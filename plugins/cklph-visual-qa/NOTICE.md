This plugin runs and adapts material from the MIT-licensed project below.
Modified by Chykalophia: `scripts/hallmark-lint` fetches and runs Hallmark's `sloplint` scripts at
the pinned commit (nothing is copied into this repo). The page-shape review in
`agents/visual-reviewer.md` rewords a few of Hallmark's page gates (44b hero fold, 34 sideways
scroll, 44d capped container, 59 section count, 58 words per screen, 85 page close, 71 heading
spacing) with our own thresholds.
